# AnimeDives

**Your gateway to binge-worthy anime — powered by the [AnimeDives](https://animedives.com/) database.**

[AnimeDives](https://animedives.com/) is a comprehensive database of completed anime series from 2000 to 2026. This app pairs that curated catalog with a fast, ad-shielded streaming experience so you can find, launch, and watch finished anime — all from one place.

## Why AnimeDives?

- **Curated completed anime** — Browse a catalog of finished series across every genre and year, from classics to the latest wrapped seasons.
- **Ad-shielded viewing** — Built-in redirect ad blocking, clutter removal, and fullscreen support so you just watch the episode.
- **Multi-provider** — Switch between built-in streaming providers or add your own custom sites.
- **Dark & light themes** — Native Material 3 theming with AMOLED-ready dark mode.
- **Search & filter** — Instantly find any anime by title or domain across all configured providers.
